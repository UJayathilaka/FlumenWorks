Five free tools for product data work

Fulmen Works is a small family of Chrome extensions. They take the repeated clicks out of uploading, checking and mapping product images and files. Each one does one job, and all of them are free.

The five tools

GXS Product Uploader

Free

Upload product images in batches. It sorts them automatically, fills the dropdowns for you, checks every upload and saves safely. You can stop instantly at any point.

v8.17.5Works on graphxserver PDB

PDF Layer Viewer

Free

Open a PDF, see every layer and switch each one on or off. Search layers, solo one, zoom in and select.

v1.12.0Opens PDFs in its own tab

Image Downloader & Zipper

Free

Paste product image links. The tool sorts them into folders and downloads everything as one ZIP.

v5.4Opens in its own tab

Mapper Toolkit

Free

Copy rows, get help inside the product dialog, close the top window with Esc and export PSD files. All in one extension.

v2.2.5Works on graphxserver pages

Master Quality Checker

Free

Make PDB links from Part IDs, run the Master Check and check the images. One tool for the whole routine.

v2.104.3Opens in its own tab
